Manage 1–10 UK rentals yourself?

We’re inviting ten iPhone users to receive a free copy of Landlord Companion, complete one real record-keeping task and tell us privately what helped or got in the way.

The task

Organise one useful record

Add one property using fictional information first if preferred, then organise a certificate or complete an inspection during the next seven days.

The feedback

Three practical questions

What worked? What was confusing? Would the app earn a place on your phone? Honest criticism is as useful as praise.

Who this is for

  • You manage between one and ten UK rental properties yourself.
  • You use an iPhone capable of running the current App Store version.
  • You can spend about 15 minutes trying one workflow during seven days.
  • You are willing to share concise, private product feedback.

What you receive

A single-use Apple promo code for the full £2.99 app. It is not a subscription and there is no payment to take part. Apple says app promo codes expire four weeks after generation and can be redeemed once.

No review is requested. Apple does not allow customers who receive a paid app through a promo code to rate or review it. The programme is for product feedback and useful completed tasks, not manufactured App Store reviews.

How to request a place

  1. Message @landlordcompanionapp on Instagram with the words “seven-day trial”.
  2. Say only whether you manage 1, 2–5 or 6–10 properties and confirm you use an iPhone. Do not send tenant names, addresses, certificates or other personal records.
  3. If a place is available, Landlord Companion will send one promo code and the three feedback questions privately.

Places are offered individually while codes remain available. A request does not guarantee a place.

Before trying the app

Use fictional records while learning. If you later add real property or tenant information, protect your phone, collect only what you need and review every export before sharing it. The app is a record-keeping tool, not legal advice.

Prefer to buy it now?

£2.99 once. No subscription.
