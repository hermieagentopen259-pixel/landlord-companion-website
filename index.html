One property or ten. The details still matter.

For the properties you know personally.

When you are the person arranging repairs, checking agreements and doing the inspections, it helps to have the records in one place. Find what you need without piecing it together from separate folders.

01

Your properties, at a glance

Today brings properties, active tenancies, upcoming rent, certificate dates and work needing attention together. Find the next record to review without searching through separate folders.

02

Properties, people & tenancies

Keep property details, tenant contacts and agreements linked. Manage occupied and vacant properties, end a tenancy and retain its history when a replacement tenancy begins.

03

Agreements & document inbox

Import agreements and certificates through Files or capture documents. On-device text recognition assists review; check extracted details yourself before saving them to a property or tenancy.

04

Certificates & reminders

Record Gas Safety, EICR, EPC and other compliance evidence. Review dates, replace certificates and use local reminders to keep important work visible.

05

Check-in, interim & check-out

Create and resume inspections. Record room conditions, meter readings, keys, notes, photographs and signatures alongside the relevant property and tenancy.

06

Repairs & follow-up

Turn inspection findings into linked repairs, keep follow-up notes and track tasks alongside the evidence that prompted them.

07

Rent records

Keep tenancy rent information and record rent status so outstanding administration remains visible. This is record-keeping, not a payment collection or banking service.

08

PDF reports & reviewed sharing

Generate inspection PDFs and prepare redacted sharing copies. Review the exact file and recipient before opening the iOS share sheet; photographs and addresses may still identify people.

09

Backup, restore & local controls

Export and restore workspace backups through Files. Choose encrypted backup for password protection; integrity checks help detect damaged archives. Use local deletion and workspace-reset controls when appropriate.

10

App lock & offline use

Use supported Face ID, Touch ID or device authentication for app lock. Core workflows run on your iPhone without a Landlord Companion account, tenant portal or developer-hosted sync service.

Private by design. Shared by choice.

A photograph is more than a file.

Inspection photos, signatures and documents can contain someone’s personal information. Keep collection purposeful, protect the device and review every export.

Understand your responsibilities

Your records stay under your control

This release does not send your property records to a Landlord Companion cloud service. Importing a photo into the app is not uploading it to us.

If you export to iCloud Drive, email or another service, that creates a separate copy governed by that destination’s access controls and terms.
