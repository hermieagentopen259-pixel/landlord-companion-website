Landlord companion

iPhone app · In development

A clearer home for your rental property records.

Landlord companion is being developed to help landlords organize properties, tenancies, inspections and important documents in one place.

Properties & tenancies

Keep the details of each property and tenancy together, with an emphasis on preserving history as tenants change.

Inspections & evidence

Bring inspection notes, photographs and reports into an organized property record.

Documents & dates

Keep certificates and their review dates close to the properties they belong to.

Designed around on-device records

The app is being built with an offline-first approach and user-controlled exports and backups. Final features and availability will be confirmed at launch.

Landlord companion is not yet available to download from this website. App Store details, support information and the app privacy policy will be published before release.
